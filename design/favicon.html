Favicon

The app icon (lucide notebook-pen) in RENCI colours: RENCI Blue #00758D, Twilight #14385F, Ultramarine #00A9C4, Quantum Pink #FF44CA, Frost #D6E8F2, Stone #666676, Slate #F3F3F8. Every part's colour can be changed; the ratio after it is its contrast against the tile, or against the light / dark sidebar when there's no tile (orange below 3:1). Each version is shown as a browser tab (16px), the sidebar header (28px) and large (64px), on light and dark. "Adaptive" follows the browser's light/dark setting here, as a favicon would; in the sidebar it would follow the app's toggle instead.
