Related papers

Since bdc-assist #73 an answer can come with two kinds of paper. Cited: the agent searched artie itself while answering, so the answer is based on them (sources.artie, as before). Related: a separate step searches afterwards and keeps up to 3 that fit; the answer isn't based on them (related.artie, new). They never overlap. Most answers will have related papers only; a question about papers can have both. Click the icons to open their lists. Titles are placeholders.
